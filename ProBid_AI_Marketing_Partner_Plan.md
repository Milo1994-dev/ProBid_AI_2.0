## The Opportunity

ProBid AI generates AI-powered construction estimates in 30 seconds. Contractors upload a photo or describe a job and get a full materials + labor breakdown with a professional PDF — instantly.

**Current numbers:**
- 11 registered users, 1 paying subscriber ($25/mo MRR)
- 1,073 homepage visits/month (organic, zero ad spend)
- 312 scraped contractor leads in the pipeline
- 63 affiliate link clicks
- The product works, the funnel is built — it just needs traffic

**The math:** There are ~3.7 million contractor businesses in the US. If we capture just 0.01% at $25/mo, that's $111,000/month in recurring revenue.

## The Three Roles

Each partner owns one channel. No overlap, clear accountability.

### Partner 1: Paid Ads (Meta + Google)

**Goal:** Drive signups through targeted ads

**Responsibilities:**
- Run Facebook/Instagram ads targeting contractors, remodelers, roofers, masons
- Run Google Search ads on keywords like "contractor estimate tool," "construction estimate software," "AI estimate generator"
- A/B test ad creatives (before/after: handwritten estimate vs ProBid PDF)
- Manage ad budget and optimize cost-per-signup
- Use the promo codes in ad copy (e.g., "Use code LAUNCH20 for 20% off")

**Targeting ideas:**
- Facebook: Job title = contractor, remodeler, mason, roofer. Interest = construction, home renovation
- Google: "free estimate software for contractors," "construction bid generator," "AI estimating tool"
- Retargeting: Anyone who visited probidcore.net but didn't sign up

**KPIs:**
- Cost per signup (target: under $15)
- Cost per paid conversion (target: under $50)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)

**Access needed:**
- Meta Business Manager (Jesse creates and adds as admin)
- Google Ads account (Jesse creates and adds as manager)
- GA4 dashboard (read access)
- Admin dashboard: probidcore.net/app/admin (key: probid-admin-2026)

## Compensation Plan

### Option A: Commission-Only (Recommended to Start)

Best for: Keeping costs low while everyone proves the model works.

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Per paid signup** | $25 one-time bonus per new paying customer |
| **Recurring commission** | 20% of monthly revenue from your referrals, paid monthly |
| **Lifetime deal bonus** | $40 per lifetime purchase ($199 x 20%) |
| **Single estimate bonus** | $1.40 per single estimate purchase ($7 x 20%) |

**How it works:**
- Each partner gets a unique affiliate link (built into the app)
- The system automatically tracks clicks, signups, and payments
- Commissions are calculated and visible in real-time on the affiliate dashboard
- Payouts monthly via PayPal, Venmo, or direct deposit

**Example earnings at scale:**
| Paying Customers | Monthly Revenue | Your 20% | Your Annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 customers | $250/mo | $50/mo | $600/yr |
| 50 customers | $1,250/mo | $250/mo | $3,000/yr |
| 200 customers | $5,000/mo | $1,000/mo | $12,000/yr |
| 500 customers | $12,500/mo | $2,500/mo | $30,000/yr |

### Option B: Base + Commission (If Budget Allows)

Best for: Ensuring consistent effort regardless of early results.

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Monthly base** | $500/mo per partner ($1,500/mo total) |
| **Recurring commission** | 10% of revenue from your referrals |
| **Performance bonus** | $500 when you hit 25 paid signups in a month |

**Vesting:** Base pay starts after a 2-week trial period. Either side can walk away in the first 2 weeks with no obligation.

### Option C: Equity Swap (Long-Term Play)

Best for: Partners who believe in the long-term vision and want ownership.

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Equity** | 2-5% per partner (vesting over 12 months) |
| **No base pay** | Commission-only during vesting period |
| **Commission rate** | 15% recurring on referred revenue |
| **Cliff** | 3-month cliff — no equity earned if they leave before 3 months |

**Important:** Get this in writing with a simple agreement. Use something like Clerky or a basic vesting agreement template.

## Legal Essentials

Before starting, get these in writing (even a simple email agreement works):

1. **Independent contractor agreement** — Each partner is a contractor, not an employee
2. **Non-compete** — Partners can't build or promote competing estimate tools during and for 6 months after
3. **IP ownership** — All content created for ProBid belongs to ProBid
4. **Termination** — Either side can end the arrangement with 14 days notice
5. **Commission terms** — How/when commissions are calculated and paid (monthly, 30-day delay for refund window)
6. **If using Option C (equity)** — Get a proper vesting agreement drafted
