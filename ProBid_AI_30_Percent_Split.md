# ProBid AI — 70/30 Revenue Split Projections

**Deal Structure:**
- Jesse keeps **70%** of all MRR
- Partners split **30%** of all MRR equally (10% each)
- Partners cover all marketing and ad expenses
- Simple, clean — no tracking individual referrals, everyone wins together

---

## Why 30% Is Fair

| Jesse Brings | Partners Bring |
|---|---|
| The product (built and maintained) | All marketing costs (ads, tools, events) |
| The infrastructure (servers, APIs, AI) | Daily effort — ads, content, outreach |
| Stripe, billing, support systems | Their time, networks, and hustle |
| Ongoing development and features | Growth — without them, MRR stays at $25 |

You built the machine. They fuel it. 70/30 is a standard founder-to-sales split for this kind of arrangement. It's fair.

## The Bottom Line

**For Jesse:** You keep 70% of everything, pay almost nothing in operating costs, and your partners fund all the growth. Over 5 years, you're looking at **$2.8M** in earnings with minimal risk.

**For the group:** $4 million in total revenue. Everyone eats. Nobody got shorted.

30% split for 3 people covering all growth costs? That's a good deal for everyone.
