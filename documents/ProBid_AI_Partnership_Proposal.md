## WHAT HAPPENS IF GOALS AREN'T MET

This section is critical. Friendships matter, but so does accountability. Here's exactly what happens:

### Yearly Performance Goals

Each year, the team is measured against the customer targets below. If the yearly goal is met, each partner earns their 2% equity for that year:

| Year | Cumulative Customer Target | Equity Earned (Per Partner) |
|------|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Year 1 | 250 paying customers | 2% |
| Year 2 | 750 paying customers | 2% |
| Year 3 | 2,000 paying customers | 2% |
| Year 4 | 4,000 paying customers | 2% |
| Year 5 | 8,000 paying customers | 2% |

### If a Partner Hits the Yearly Goal
- They earn their full 2% equity for that year
- Business continues as planned

### If a Partner Hits 75-99% of the Yearly Goal
- They earn only **1%** equity for that year instead of 2%
- A corrective action plan is created together for the next year

### If a Partner Hits Below 75% of the Yearly Goal
- **No equity earned** for that year — it's forfeited permanently and does not carry over
- A formal conversation happens to determine if the partner should continue
- If performance stays below 75% for **two consecutive years**, the partnership can be terminated

### If a Partner Quits or Stops Showing Up
- They keep only the equity they've already earned up to that point
- All unvested equity returns to the Founder
- The revenue share stops immediately
- NDA and non-compete remain in effect for 12 months
- Their role is either reassigned to a new partner or redistributed

### If a Partner Exceeds the Yearly Goal by 25% or More
- They earn their full 2% equity **plus a 0.5% bonus** for that year
- This rewards outperformance and keeps top performers motivated
- Maximum possible equity per partner with bonuses: 12.5% over 5 years

### The 6-Month Check-In

At the halfway point of Year 1 (Month 6), there will be a progress review. If the team has fewer than 75 paying customers by Month 6, either side can walk away cleanly:

- No equity earned
- No hard feelings
- NDA stays in effect
- Everyone moves on

This protects the Founder from partners who talk big but don't follow through, and it protects the partners from committing to something that isn't working.

### Hours Tracking

Each partner is expected to log their weekly hours and activities in a shared tracker. This isn't about micromanaging — it's about transparency. If someone claims they're putting in 15 hours a week but has nothing to show for it, the numbers don't lie.

### What This Means in Plain English

- **Do the work, hit the goals** = you earn your equity and get paid
- **Fall behind but show effort** = you get a chance to catch up
- **Don't show up or don't produce** = you lose your equity and your spot
- **Crush it** = you earn even more

Nobody rides for free. The plan works when everyone pulls their weight.
