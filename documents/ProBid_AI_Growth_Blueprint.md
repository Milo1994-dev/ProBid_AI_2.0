# ProBid AI — Growth Partner Blueprint
## Roadmap to 1,000 Paid Subscribers

**Company**: ProBid AI (probidcore.net)
**Product**: AI-Powered Construction Estimates for Small Contractors
**Date**: April 12, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

This blueprint outlines the strategic plan to grow ProBid AI from its current stage to 1,000 paying subscribers within 18 months. The plan is structured in quarterly phases with measurable KPIs, specific tactics, and clear ownership between the Founder and Growth Partner.

**Revenue Target at 1,000 Subscribers**: ~$31,000–$37,000 MRR

## Quarter 1 (Months 1–3): Foundation & First Conversions

**Subscriber Goal**: 50–80 paying subscribers

### 1.1 — Niche Selection & Positioning
- **Pick ONE primary trade** to dominate first (recommended: Masonry/Tuckpointing — highest estimate values, underserved market)
- **Pick 3–5 target metro areas** to focus all ad spend and outreach
- Refine messaging: "Get a professional estimate in 2 minutes — not 2 hours"
- Create before/after case studies from real estimates

### 1.2 — Paid Acquisition Setup
- **Facebook/Instagram Ads**
  - Target: Contractors, small business owners, construction workers
  - Interest targeting: Procore, HomeAdvisor, Angi, contractor tools
  - Lookalike audiences from existing users
  - Ad creative: Video demo showing estimate generation in real-time
  - Budget: $1,500–$3,000/month
  - Goal: $15–$30 CAC (customer acquisition cost)
- **Google Ads**
  - Search campaigns: "construction estimate software", "contractor bidding tool", "tuckpointing estimate"
  - Local service ads in target metros
  - Budget: $1,000–$2,000/month
  - Conversion tracking already wired up (Meta Pixel + Google Ads integration in place)

### 1.3 — Organic & Community
- **Facebook Groups**: Join 10–15 contractor groups, provide value, soft-promote
- **Reddit**: r/Construction, r/Contractors, r/smallbusiness — helpful posts with subtle mentions
- **YouTube**: 2–3 demo videos showing estimate generation for different job types
- **TikTok/Reels**: Short-form content showing the "magic moment" — photo to estimate in seconds

### 1.4 — Outreach Engine Activation
- Activate the built-in lead scraper (Google Places API) for target metros
- Configure outreach email sequences on outreach.probidcore.net subdomain
- Start with 20 emails/day, warm up to 50/day by end of quarter
- Three-touch email sequence already built — optimize subject lines and CTAs

### 1.5 — Conversion Optimization
- Monitor free-to-paid conversion funnel
- A/B test upgrade modal messaging (urgency vs. value vs. social proof)
- Track $7 single estimate → Pro subscription upgrade path
- Ensure onboarding walkthrough is smooth and drives first estimate quickly

### Q1 KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Total signups | 1,000–2,000 |
| Paying subscribers | 50–80 |
| $7 purchases | 100+ |
| CAC (blended) | < $40 |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 3%+ |
| Monthly ad spend | $2,500–$5,000 |
| Outreach emails sent | 1,500+ |

## Growth Partner Responsibilities

1. **Paid Acquisition**: Own all ad campaigns (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn), budget allocation, creative testing, and optimization
2. **Outreach**: Manage email outreach campaigns, lead scraper configuration, and cold outreach messaging
3. **Affiliates**: Recruit, onboard, and manage affiliate partners; create affiliate marketing materials
4. **Content & Social**: Create and distribute content across social channels, YouTube, TikTok
5. **Partnerships**: Identify and pursue strategic partnerships with industry players
6. **Analytics**: Weekly reporting on all growth metrics, funnel analysis, and recommendations
7. **Conversion Optimization**: A/B test landing pages, upgrade flows, and pricing presentation

## Founder Responsibilities

1. **Product**: Maintain and improve the platform, fix bugs, ship new features
2. **AI Quality**: Ensure estimate accuracy and AI model improvements
3. **Customer Support**: Handle user issues and feedback
4. **Finance**: Manage Stripe billing, payroll, and financial operations
5. **Legal**: Contracts, terms of service, privacy compliance
6. **Infrastructure**: Server uptime, database management, deployments
