## ARTICLE 1 — PURPOSE

The Founder is the sole owner and operator of ProBid AI, a SaaS platform providing AI-powered construction estimates to contractors, operating at probidcore.net ("the Company"). ## ARTICLE 5 — PERFORMANCE-BASED EQUITY VESTING

### 5.1 Total Equity Pool
The Founder agrees to allocate a total of **____% equity** in ProBid AI / ProBidCore LLC to the Partner, subject to the performance-based vesting schedule outlined below.

### 5.2 Vesting Structure
Equity shall vest **quarterly** over **six (6) quarters** (18 months), with each quarter's vesting contingent upon the Partner meeting or exceeding the defined subscriber milestone for that quarter. No equity vests automatically — **every share must be earned**.

### 5.3 Quarterly Milestones & Equity Vesting Schedule

| Quarter | Period | Cumulative Subscriber Target | Equity Earned | Cumulative Equity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Months 1–3 | 80 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |
| Q2 | Months 4–6 | 250 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |
| Q3 | Months 7–9 | 500 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |
| Q4 | Months 10–12 | 750 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |
| Q5 | Months 13–15 | 900 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |
| Q6 | Months 16–18 | 1,000 paying subscribers | ___% | ___% |

### 5.4 Definition of "Paying Subscriber"
A "paying subscriber" is defined as a user who:

- Has an active, non-cancelled Pro ($25/month) or Business ($55/month) subscription, OR
- Has an active annual subscription in good standing
- Lifetime purchases ($199) count as 1 subscriber for milestone purposes
- Single estimate purchases ($7) do NOT count toward subscriber milestones
- Users in a free trial period do NOT count until they convert to a paid plan
- Users with failed payments or past-due subscriptions do NOT count

### 5.5 Measurement & Verification
- Subscriber counts will be measured from the Company's Stripe dashboard and internal admin analytics on the **last day of each quarter**
- Both Parties shall have access to verify subscriber counts
- The Founder will provide a written subscriber report within 5 business days of each quarter's end
- Any disputes over subscriber counts shall be resolved by reference to Stripe's billing records, which shall be considered the definitive source

### 5.6 Partial Performance
- If the Partner achieves **at least 75%** of a quarterly milestone, they shall receive **50%** of that quarter's equity allocation
- If the Partner achieves **less than 75%** of a quarterly milestone, **no equity vests** for that quarter
- Unvested equity from a missed quarter **does not carry forward** — it is forfeited permanently
- If the Partner **exceeds** a quarterly milestone by 25% or more, they may receive an accelerated vesting bonus of **25% additional equity** for that quarter (drawn from the total equity pool)

### 5.7 Cliff Period
There is no cliff period. However, Q1 serves as a proving ground — if the Partner fails to achieve at least 75% of the Q1 milestone (60 subscribers), either party may terminate this Agreement with no equity vested and no further obligations beyond the NDA.

## ARTICLE 9 — TERM & TERMINATION

### 9.1 Term
This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and continue for **eighteen (18) months**, unless terminated earlier as provided below.

### 9.2 Termination by Either Party
Either party may terminate this Agreement with **30 days' written notice** for any reason.

### 9.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party:

- Commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice
- Engages in fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct
- Discloses Confidential Information in violation of Article 3
- Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

### 9.4 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:

- The Partner retains any equity that has already vested as of the termination date
- All unvested equity is forfeited and returns to the Founder
- The NDA obligations in Articles 2, 3, and 4 survive termination
- The Partner shall return all Confidential Information per Section 3.2
- Any outstanding marketing expenses incurred with Founder approval shall be settled within 30 days
