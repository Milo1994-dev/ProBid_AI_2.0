# ProBid AI — Final Revenue Split & Equity Plan

**Structure:**
- **Jesse: 60%** of MRR
- **Partner group: 35%** of MRR (split 3 ways = ~11.67% each)
- **Business reinvestment fund: 5%** of MRR (marketing budget once revenue flows)
- **Equity bonus:** Each partner earns **2% ownership per year** if annual goals are hit (max 6% each / 18% total by Year 5)
- Partners cover all marketing expenses until the 5% fund kicks in

---

## How the Equity Bonus Works

| Year | Goal to Hit | Each Partner Earns | Cumulative Equity (Each) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 260 paying customers / $7,800 MRR by Dec | 2% | 2% |
| Year 2 | 940 paying customers / $28,200 MRR by Dec | 2% | 4% |
| Year 3 | 2,200 paying customers / $66,000 MRR by Dec | 2% | 6% |
| Year 4 | 4,050 paying customers / $121,500 MRR by Dec | +0% (capped) | 6% |
| Year 5 | 6,100 paying customers / $183,000 MRR by Dec | +0% (capped) | 6% |

- Equity vests at end of each year only if the goal is met
- If a partner leaves before year-end, they forfeit that year's 2%
- Previously vested equity stays with them
- Max total equity given away: 18% (6% x 3 partners)
- Jesse retains minimum 82% ownership

## What to Put in Writing

You need a simple agreement covering:

1. **Revenue split:** 60% Jesse / 35% partners / 5% business fund — based on MRR collected (after Stripe fees and refunds)
2. **Equity vesting:** 2% per partner per year, contingent on hitting the annual customer/MRR goal, vests December 31 each year
3. **Cliff:** Partner must complete the full calendar year to earn that year's equity
4. **Cap:** 6% max per partner, 18% total
5. **Expense responsibility:** Partners cover all marketing and advertising costs; the 5% business fund supplements but doesn't reimburse
6. **Payout schedule:** Monthly, within 10 days of month-end
7. **Termination:** 30-day notice; vested equity stays, unvested is forfeited
8. **Non-compete:** Can't promote or build competing estimate tools during engagement and 12 months after
9. **IP:** All marketing materials created belong to ProBid AI

Get this signed before anyone starts working. A lawyer can draft this for $300-500, or use a template from Clerky or Rocket Lawyer.
