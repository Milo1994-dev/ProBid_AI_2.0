# ProBid AI — 5-Year Revenue & Earnings Projections

**Assumptions:**
- Partners cover all marketing/ad expenses (not deducted from Jesse's revenue)
- Partners earn 20% recurring commission on customers they bring in
- Jesse keeps 80% of all partner-referred revenue + 100% of organic revenue
- Pricing: $25/mo Pro, $55/mo Business, $7 single estimate, $199 lifetime (limited launch promo — capped at 100 buyers; sold out or removed once cap is hit)
- Blended average revenue per paying user: ~$30/mo (mix of Pro + Business)
- Churn rate: 8%/mo Year 1 → 5%/mo by Year 5 (improves as product matures)
- Organic signups grow as SEO, word-of-mouth, and viral PDFs compound

## The Pitch to Your Partners

> "You cover the marketing costs — ad spend, content tools, event fees. I built the product, the infrastructure, and the entire sales funnel. You earn 20% recurring commission on every customer you bring in. That means every customer you sign up keeps paying you month after month. By Year 3, you're each making $40K+ just from commissions. By Year 5, it's six figures each. And you're building equity in relationships and a book of business you own."

**Why this works for everyone:**
- **Jesse:** Zero marketing costs. You keep 80% of referred revenue and 100% of organic. Your only costs are server/API fees (~$200-500/mo at scale)
- **Partners:** Low barrier to entry. No upfront investment beyond their own time and ad budget. Recurring income that compounds every month — customers they sign in Month 1 are still paying them in Month 60
- **The math is simple:** Every $25/mo customer = $5/mo to the partner who brought them. Get 100 customers? That's $500/mo passive income. Get 500? That's $2,500/mo. It only goes up.

## Key Risks & Honest Disclaimers

1. **Year 1 is the hardest** — partners may spend more than they earn. Set expectations upfront
2. **Churn is real** — not every customer stays. These projections assume improving retention
3. **Competition** — other AI estimate tools will emerge. Speed to market matters NOW
4. **Ad costs fluctuate** — Meta/Google CPMs can spike. Organic content is the hedge
5. **These are projections, not guarantees** — they assume consistent effort from all 4 people

The difference between this working and not working is simple: **consistent daily effort for 90 days.** After that, the recurring revenue flywheel takes over.
